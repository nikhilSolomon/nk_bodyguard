nk_bodyguard
Bodyguard Center

Hireable NPC bodyguards for FiveM with a modern command panel. Players sign contracts at the Bodyguard Agency; admins command for free. Formations, chauffeur, escort car, air support and marked-target attacks, all without a single teleport.

Overview

Live stats, squad mode, quick orders and an event log. Admins recruit for free; citizens see their Agency contracts.

Squad & individual orders

Health, armour, kills, tier and rank per guard. Attack, Go to, Follow, Hold, Send home, Medic, Make driver.

Formation editor

Loose, Circle, Wedge, Line or drag your own custom formation on a 1 m grid and apply it.

Vehicle & driving

Seat assignment with a chauffeur seat, five driving styles, escort car and air support.

Settings

Admin tools (invincible, regen, reinforce, accuracy), minimap blips, panel position and size.

Bodyguard Agency

Four contract tiers with price, loadout, stats and outfit choice. Contracts persist until the guard is killed or dismissed.

Citizen view

The same panel for players: paid services with prices, Agency GPS, no admin tools.

Features

Agency & contracts

NPC manager with blip. Tiers, prices, outfits and names are config. Contracts live in nk_bodyguard_contracts and come back after relog.

Roles

Admins (ESX group or command.bodyguard ace) recruit free and get every toggle; citizens pay for escort, air and medic.

Veterancy

Kills are saved on the contract. Veteran at 5 kills, Legend at 15, with accuracy and armour bonuses.

Mark & attack / Move to

Aim in-game, press E. Peds are fought until dead, vehicles until wrecked. Squad-wide or per guard.

Natural movement

Guards walk, run and sprint into formation, chase your car and board when it stops. No teleports.

Integrations

Framework bridge (ESX), notification hook (native, esx, ox_lib, okok, mythic, custom), phone exports and events.
